How general businesses lose money
  • Poor Cash Flow Management: Waiting too long for receivables while payables are due.
  • Over-expansion: Expanding operations or hiring too quickly before revenue supports it.
  • Pricing Issues: Underpricing products to gain market share but destroying margins.
  • Ignoring Small Expenses: Death by a thousand cuts—subscriptions and minor fees add up.
  • Lack of Emergency Fund: No buffer for economic downturns or supply chain shocks.
